Follow one payment.

Send 1,500 ZERO to a friend and see what each part of the system learns along the way.

  1. Sign once

    Your wallet signs one message and your private keys are derived from it. Nothing new to write down: same wallet, same keys, on any device.

  2. Prove it on your device

    The app writes a zero-knowledge proof right here: you own notes worth at least 1,500 ZERO, and the new notes add up. The proof shows that and nothing more.

  3. The fee comes from your notes

    A bundler carries the payment to the chain. The network fee is a flat amount of ZERO taken from your notes, so your address never pays gas and never appears.

  4. The vault keeps ciphertext

    The vault checks the proof and stores only what it must: a nullifier so no note is spent twice, the new note commitments, and each note encrypted for its owner.

  5. Only your friend can read it

    Their viewing key opens the note: 1,500 ZERO, ready to spend or sell. If you ever need to, you can share a proof of this one payment and nothing else.

Every trade pays private holders.

A fee on every ZERO trade, wherever it happens, buys ZERO for the vault. Every note's share of the vault grows with it.

Questions.

The details are in the docs.

What makes ZERO a privacy token?

ZERO is an ERC-20 that trades like any other. Shielded, it lives in the ZeroTrace vault as an encrypted note: the amount, the sender and the recipient are hidden, and moving it takes a zero-knowledge proof instead of a public transfer.

Is buying ZERO private?

The purchase itself is visible on-chain, like any trade. What comes after is not: buy in the app and the tokens land straight in a private note, and the payments and sales you make from it do not link back to you.

Where does the holder yield come from?

From trading fees, not from new tokens. Part of the fee on every ZERO trade is harvested: it keeps the gas for private payments topped up and buys ZERO for the vault, and the network fees private payments pay go to the vault too. It depends on how much ZERO trades, so nothing about it is guaranteed.

Can anyone freeze or take my notes?

No. The vault has no admin key, no pause and no upgrade path. A note moves only with its owner's signature, and nobody else holds that key, the team included.

What do I need to start?

A browser wallet such as MetaMask, on Robinhood Chain, and some ETH to buy ZERO. You sign one message to create your privacy keys; the first time, one small transaction registers them so others can pay you.

Can I prove a payment if I have to?

Yes. Every private send has a proof of payment you can share as a link. Whoever opens it can check, against the chain, who received that one payment and how much. It reveals nothing else about you.

What if this website goes away?

Your keys come from your wallet's signature, not from this site, and the vault contract is public and immutable. Private payments go through standard ERC-4337 bundlers, so no single server stands between you and your notes.
